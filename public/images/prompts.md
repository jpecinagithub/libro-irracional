# Prompts para generar las 6 ilustraciones (estilo B Moderna limpia — duotono minimal, fondo blanco, línea fina slate)

Usar Stable Diffusion XL / Flux (open-source) o similar. Todas en formato cuadrado 1024x1024, estilo vector minimal, paleta slate-900 + amber-500 acento, mucho whitespace.

1. **Hero — Cruz luminosa**  
   `minimal geometric cross on white background, thin slate line, soft amber glow circle behind, modern editorial, whitespace, vector, duotone`

2. **Bienaventuranzas — Monte**  
   `minimal mountain with tiny silhouettes listening, thin line art, slate on white, amber sun dot, modern icon style`

3. **Libertad — Puertas abiertas**  
   `minimal open doors in a labyrinth wall opening to light, thin line, slate-900, amber light rays, clean geometric`

4. **Herida — Jardín con grieta de luz**  
   `minimal garden abstract with a crack of light through soil, thin line art, slate, amber crack glow, editorial`

5. **Cruz — Kolbe / entrega**  
   `minimal hands offering light in dark, abstract, thin line, slate and amber, compassionate, whitespace`

6. **Resurrección — Sepulcro vacío**  
   `minimal empty tomb at dawn, open door, soft amber sunrise, thin line art, slate, hope, minimal`

Tip: generar y exportar a `webp` 800w, colocar en `public/images/` como `01-hero.webp` etc., y referenciar en componentes si se desea.
